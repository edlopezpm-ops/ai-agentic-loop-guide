Research date: July 2, 2026.

## Practical Takeaways for This Demo

- A Solution Engineer is evaluated less as a pure builder and more as a trusted translator between customer pain, product capability, technical feasibility, and sales momentum.
- The app should prove fast synthesis: "I can understand the customer, shape the story, and build a credible demo artifact quickly."
- Current Salesforce language should appear naturally: Customer 360, AI, data, trust, Agentforce/Data 360, Service/Sales workflows, Slack collaboration, and measurable business value.
- Avoid turning the presentation into a technology inventory. The strongest story is discovery -> hypothesis -> solution narrative -> proof.
- Puja is likely to value clarity, leadership, communication, and business judgment more than low-level implementation detail.

## Demo Narrative

The app should quietly answer four hiring-manager questions:

1. Can Ed gather requirements before jumping to a solution?
2. Can Ed communicate technical ideas to non-technical stakeholders?
3. Can Ed handle pressure and difficult internal/customer dynamics?
4. Can Ed create a polished customer-facing artifact quickly with modern AI-assisted workflow?

## Messaging Anchor

"I help customers buy confidence: confidence that the problem is understood, the solution is realistic, and the team can deliver."
